# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

React, Vite, TypeScript, SVG and CSS animation. The first release is a static site with a configurable Vite base path and no required backend.

## Users

People attending OpenTenBase or student-club activities who may have no database, programming, or distributed-systems background. They need to understand the system by acting on visible data-flow problems, not by studying terminology first.

## Product Purpose

TenDispatch turns OpenTenBase's coordinator, data nodes, data distribution, query routing, replication tables, and global transaction coordination into one short interactive data-logistics experience. Success means a first-time player can finish the flow, see the consequences of each decision, receive a credible score, and explain why distributed scheduling matters.

## Positioning

The player operates one continuous data logistics center: data arrives, CN dispatches it, DN warehouses visibly load up, and professional concepts are named only after their effects have been seen.

## Operating Context

The experience is primarily shown on PCs and laptops at an in-person activity booth. It must remain usable on phones, run from static files, preserve local rankings between sessions, and allow anonymous nicknames.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Continuous tutorial, sharding, query, replication, GTM, final dispatch, report, and ranking flow.
- Behavior tracking through one EventTracker and persistence only through repository abstractions.
- No SQL, code editor, 3D engine, physics simulation, game-over state, required login, or required API.
- CN, three DN nodes, and GTM retain their real OpenTenBase architectural meaning.
- The first version uses LocalRepository; ApiRepository remains an explicit future integration seam.

## Brand Commitments

The product name is TenDispatch with the Chinese subtitle “OpenTenBase 数据调度中心”. The maker identity is “电子科技大学成都学院开放原子开源社团”. The club appears as a remote communication NPC, not the player or an interruptive promotion. The supplied club avatar at `image/科成-开放原子开源社团.png` must remain intact and replaceable through configuration.

## Evidence on Hand

The implementation brief in the user-provided pasted text is the product authority. The supplied club avatar is the identity asset. No production API, public leaderboard, or real player data is currently available and none may be fabricated as real.

## Product Principles

- Show the effect before naming the term.
- Let every decision change the visible logistics center.
- Keep the full journey short, forgiving, and continuous.
- Make OpenTenBase architecture the mechanism rather than decoration.
- Record learning behavior without requiring personal identity.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

All essential state must be expressed by labels and values in addition to color and motion. Keyboard focus, reduced-motion preferences, readable contrast, and responsive layouts are required.
